We benchmark the ODE baseline and the analytic HiPPO-SVGP on the solar stream under two timing protocols. In `kernel_only`, we measure only the construction of the interdomain kernel terms used by the variational objective. In `full_update`, we measure a complete variational update step consisting of ELBO evaluation, backpropagation, and one optimizer step. For the runtime-versus-$T$ study we fix the solar observation set and vary the effective stream length $T \in \{100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000\}$ by changing the number of HiPPO discretization steps over the same time span. For the runtime-versus-$M$ study we fix $T=1000$ and sweep the basis size $M \in \{8, 16, 32, 64, 128\}$. Across all runs we keep the solar data source, kernel family, random feature count, hyperparameters, dtype, device, and random seed fixed. Each plotted point reports the median wall-clock runtime over 20 measured repeats after 5 warmup iterations, with CUDA synchronization immediately before and after each timing block. We report the raw per-repeat timings and aggregated summary statistics with the benchmark artifacts, and we restrict our claims to the measured wall-clock behavior shown in the figures.
